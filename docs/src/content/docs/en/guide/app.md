## Android app

1. Get the APK file from the admin (or the link they give you) and open it on the phone.
2. Allow installing from that source when Android asks (a one-time setting).
3. Open **Beer Rates** and log in. You stay logged in until you log out.

Updates come as a new APK installed over the old one; your login and data stay.

## iPhone

Open [øl.cer06.no](https://xn--l-4ga.cer06.no) in Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen, works offline and can get notifications (iOS 16.4 or newer).

## Without a connection

- The last drinks you saw are still shown.
- **Save** stores new drinks (with photo) on the phone. They're shown at the top of the list as *Waiting to upload*.
- They upload by themselves when the connection is back, when you open the app, or when you tap **Send now**.
- If the server refuses one (for example a missing name), it's shown in red with the reason; delete it or fix it.
- Searching the online databases and reading barcodes from the databases need a connection; label reading works offline once it has been used once.

## Notifications

Under **Profile → Notifications**, turn them on for the phone or browser, and choose what you want:

- someone cheers one of your drinks,
- someone else logs a drink,
- (admins) a new user signs up.
