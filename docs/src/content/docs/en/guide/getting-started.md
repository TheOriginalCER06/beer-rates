Beer Rates is at **[øl.cer06.no](https://xn--l-4ga.cer06.no)**. When public viewing is on (the default), anyone with the link can browse drinks, stats and the calendar without logging in.

## Get an account

- Tap **Log in → Create one**. Depending on the admin's settings, the account works at once or waits for an admin to approve it; you get an email when it's approved.
- If self sign-up is off, ask an admin to create an account for you.
- Accounts from the old Beer Rates were moved over with their passwords: log in as before. Add an email address under **Profile** so you can reset your password later.

## Get the app

| Phone | How |
|---|---|
| **Android** | Install the Beer Rates app (APK) from the admin, see [The app and offline use](/docs/en/guide/app/). |
| **iPhone** | Open the site in Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen like an app. |
| **Computer** | Just use the browser. |

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Browse everything, cheer drinks. |
| **Contributor** | Also add drinks, and change or delete their own. |
| **Admin** | Everything, including users and site settings (**Manage**). |
