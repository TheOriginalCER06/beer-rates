## The list

- Search name, brewery, style, notes, country or place.
- The chips filter by category; the filter button sorts (date, rating, name, ABV, added) and filters by person, rating and "I'd have it again".
- More drinks load as you scroll.

## A drink

Tap a drink for the big photo (tap it again for full screen), all details and other times the same drink was logged. **🍻 Cheers** raises a glass to someone else's drink; they can get a notification. Your own drinks have **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Calendar

One month at a time, with a coloured dot per drink (colour = category). Tap a day to see what was had.

## Statistics

For everyone, yourself or one person, and for all years or one year: average rating, rating spread, who logs most, categories, months, styles, countries, breweries and the highest rated drinks.

## Export

**Profile → Export** downloads your drinks (or everyone's) as Excel or CSV. In the Android app, the phone's share sheet opens so you can save the file or send it on.
