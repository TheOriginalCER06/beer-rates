Tap the round **+** at the bottom (on a computer: **Add drink** at the top). Only the **name** and the **rating** (1–10) are required.

## Photo

1. **Camera** takes a photo, **Gallery** picks one you have.
2. The **crop** screen opens: drag to move, pinch (or use the slider) to zoom, **↻** rotates. Pick a shape: 4:5 (default, good for bottles and glasses), 1:1, 3:4, 9:16 or the whole picture.
3. **Use photo**. The app warns if the photo looks blurry or dark. Tap the crop icon on the photo to crop again.

Photos are made smaller on the phone (usually 200–500 kB) before they're uploaded, and the server removes the GPS position and other camera data from them.

## Barcode

**Barcode** scans the code on the bottle or can. The app then:

- fills in name, brewery, ABV and country from [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/), when the product is there,
- shows if you or others have logged a drink with the same code before.

On Android the scan uses the phone's own scanner. On iPhone and in the browser the camera opens in the app; you can also read the code from a photo or type the number.

## Read the label

After taking a photo, **Read the label** reads the text on it, on the phone (nothing is sent anywhere). The first time it downloads the reading engine (~20 MB). Then:

- The lines that look like a name are shown: tap one to use it as the name.
- An ABV like "4.7 % vol" fills in the ABV field.
- The text is searched in the online databases, so you can pick the right drink with a clean name.

Crop close to the label first: that makes the reading much better.

## Online databases

**Search online databases** looks up the name (or what you type) in free databases: Open Food Facts (beer, wine and other bottles), OpenBreweryDB (breweries), TheCocktailDB (cocktails) and Vivino (wine). Tap a result to fill in the form. If you have no photo, the database's picture is used.

## While typing

- **Had before** shows earlier drinks with a similar name.
- Brewery, country, style and place suggest what has been used before.
- If the app is closed while you're taking a photo, the form is still filled in when you come back.

## No connection?

You can save anyway: the drink is kept on the phone and uploaded by itself later. See [The app and offline use](/en/guide/app/).
